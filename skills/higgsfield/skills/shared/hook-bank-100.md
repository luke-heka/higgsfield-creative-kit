# 100-Hook Bank (Short-Form Video Openers)

Reference library of 100 viral-tested hooks for short-form video (Reels,
TikTok, Shorts, UGC ads). Organized by hook archetype. Use to seed
multi-chunk UGC scripts, carousel slide-1 hooks, and reel openers.

Loaded by `higgsfield-ugc-ads`, `higgsfield-marketing-studio`,
`higgsfield-content-factory`, `higgsfield-viral-replicator`.

> **How to use:** pick an archetype that matches the angle of the content,
> then pick a hook line and substitute the bracketed variables. Always
> voice-grade through `content-engine` before locking — some patterns
> need rephrasing for your brand voice (no em dashes, no "game-changer", no
> outcome promises).

## Archetype 1 — Problem-Aware (10)

For ads/content aimed at audiences already feeling the pain.

1. "If you've ever tried to [TASK], you already know this isn't normal."
2. "Stop scrolling if you've been [SYMPTOM] for more than [TIMEFRAME]."
3. "The reason you're still [FAILING AT X] has nothing to do with effort."
4. "[NUMBER] hours into [TASK] and I still can't figure out why [Y]."
5. "If [PROBLEM] is ruining your [AREA OF LIFE], you're not alone."
6. "Three years of [PROBLEM]. Here's what actually moved the needle."
7. "I almost gave up on [GOAL] because of [SPECIFIC THING NO ONE TALKS ABOUT]."
8. "Why does no one tell you that [COMMON TRUTH ABOUT PROBLEM]?"
9. "The [PROBLEM] nobody warned me about when I started [PURSUIT]."
10. "POV: it's [DAY/TIME] and you're still dealing with [PROBLEM]."

## Selr AI Voice-Grading Rules (apply BEFORE locking any hook)

- **No em dashes.** Use comma or full stop.
- **No "game-changer", "10x", "crushing it", "killing it", "secret sauce", "level up", "unlock", "transform"** — pipe through `content-engine` blocklist.
- **No outcome guarantees** ("you WILL...", "guaranteed to...", "the only way to...") — a hard brand rule.
- **No "drop in" / "swing past" / "say hi" CTAs.**
- **No personal-life-in-marketing** — hooks like "my [FAMILY MEMBER] asked me" are off-limits for Selr AI brand. Use business-context substitute ("a client asked me", "a workshop attendee asked me").
- **AU English** ("colour" not "color", "optimise" not "optimize", "specialise" not "specialize").

## Composition Tips

- **Mix archetypes** across a content series. Don't ship 10 "X mistakes" hooks in a row.
- **Pair the hook with the visual cold open.** A "Watch this" hook needs an immediate visual payoff in frame 1, not a 2s logo intro.
- **Hook length:** keep under 8 words for IG Reels (mobile cutoff), under 12 for TikTok/Shorts (more lenient).
- **Front-load the most surprising/specific word.** "Three years of insomnia..." beats "I had a problem for three years..."
- **Test 2-3 hooks against the same body.** Same script, different opener. Lowest CPM wins.
