## Campaign Goal

**Launch with cart open and cart close.** Drive cohort fills inside
the open-close window, build email list from the lead magnet for
the next cohort, position the coach as the named authority in the
niche.

- **Anchor metric:** Paid enrolments by cart close
  (`{{CART_CLOSE_DATE}}`)
- **Secondary metric:** Email opt-ins from lead magnet during the
  pre-launch window
- **Tertiary metric:** Show-up rate to the pre-cart-open masterclass
  / workshop / webinar (`{{MASTERCLASS_DATE}}`)

## ICP Guesses (To Validate in Stage 1)

The Stage 1 prompt will narrow these. Typical coach segment shapes:

1. **Course-fatigued operator**, REPEAT primary. Bought 3+ courses,
   finished none. Wants accountability and a cohort, not another
   library. Buys when one operator they trust says it actually
   moved the needle.
2. **The "almost there" practitioner**, AMPLIFIER. Already at the
   `{{SPECIFIC_PLATEAU}}` the program addresses. Drives word-of-mouth
   to their own peer group when results hit.
3. **The new-to-niche learner**, NEITHER. Just discovered the niche,
   needs to be educated before buying. Long pre-cart-open sequence
   needed. Low immediate-buy rate, high LTV if nurtured.

## Channel Emphasis (Coach / Course Default)

Stage 2 builds the channel plan. Defaults for this vertical:

- **Primary:** Email (the warmed list does most of the work in cohort
  launches) + YouTube long-form (the depth + receipts platform) +
  organic Instagram (the receipts + behind-the-scenes platform)
- **Secondary:** LinkedIn for B2B coach niches, X / Twitter for
  build-in-public coach niches, Threads for newsletter-led coaches
- **Skip by default (in launches):** TikTok (high-volume, low cohort-
  fit, optional for top-of-funnel only), paid Meta (run separately
  as evergreen, not inside the cart-open spike)

## Visual Direction (Anchor)

- **Setting:** Coach's home office, co-working space, hotel lobby,
  walking outdoors with phone selfie. NOT a styled studio. NOT a
  rented luxury prop (no Lambo, no penthouse). Lived-in, working
  environments.
- **Subjects:** The coach (always). Optional: real clients on
  testimonial cuts (with written permission). Wardrobe = elevated
  casual (good t-shirt, blazer over tee, no power suit).
- **Lighting:** Natural light from a window, ring light for talking-
  head only if the window light is bad. Warm, soft, lived-in.
- **Camera energy:**
  - For talking-head: still cam, mid-shot, leaning slightly in.
  - For screen-record-teach: full screen with founder webcam in
    corner (Loom-style), arrows + circles drawn on the framework.
  - For walking-and-talking: handheld phone selfie outdoors or hotel
    lobby.
- **Color palette anchors:** Defaults to the coach's existing brand
  palette (captured at intake). Texture = real, not stylised.
- **Product visibility:** The coach IS the product. Hero is the
  coach's face. Method framework (whiteboard / slide / Figma frame)
  is the secondary visual hero.

## Voice Direction (Anchor)

- **Tone words:** high-conviction, story-led, slightly-fast, like
  they're explaining the thing they're obsessed with
- **Phrases to use:**
  - "Here's what nobody told me about `{{NICHE}}`"
  - "I figured this out after `{{NUMBER}}` `{{ATTEMPTS_OR_YEARS}}`"
  - "My client `{{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}` did `{{SPECIFIC_THING}}` and
    `{{SPECIFIC_RESULT}}`"
  - "Watch what happens when you `{{ACTION}}` instead of `{{COMMON_BELIEF}}`"
  - "I built `{{METHOD_NAME}}` because `{{ORIGIN_PAIN}}`"
- **Phrases banned (Coach-specific, compliance + trust):**
  - "Make `{{$X}}` in `{{TIMEFRAME}}`" (FTC + Meta will reject)
  - "Passive income" without a real receipts breakdown
  - "Guaranteed results" (banned universally)
  - "Limited spots" without a real cohort cap and date
  - "DM me 'INFO'" (TikTok demotes DM-bait CTAs in 2026)
  - "Quit your 9 to 5" (over-promised, demoted by every platform)
  - "Wolf of Wall Street" anything (rented luxury energy)
  - "Secret sauce" / "hidden growth lever" / "unlock"

- **Universal bans (apply across all packs):**
  - Em dashes
  - Outcome guarantees of any specific dollar figure or percentage
  - Refund / money-back / 30-day guarantee language in copy
  - Personal life of operator beyond the chosen brand-origin story

## Hard Constraints (Coach / Course Vertical)

- **Never promise specific income outcomes.** No "make $10K/mo in
  30 days", "first $100K", "6-figure launch in 90 days". FTC will
  flag. Meta will reject ads.
- **No rented luxury props.** No Lambo in the driveway, no rented
  penthouse, no "look at my Rolex" energy. Trust-killer in 2026.
- **No fake scarcity.** "Only 3 spots left" must be REAL. If the
  cohort cap is 30 and 27 have sold, "3 spots left" is fine. If
  the cap is theoretical, don't use scarcity copy.
- **No paid testimonials presented as organic.** FTC disclosure
  rules apply universally.
- **No "guru" energy.** No Tony-Robbins stage poses, no rooftop
  drone shots over a Miami skyline.
- **No DM-bait CTAs in 2026.** TikTok algorithmically demotes "DM
  me 'INFO'" posts. Use a free resource CTA with a real URL instead.
- **No "I changed my life, you can too" generic promises.** Lead
  with the specific named result, the specific client, the specific
  receipts.

## Campaign Window Default

- **Pre-launch (warmup):** 14 days before cart open (T-14 to T-1),
  lead magnet promo, free training delivery, story-arc emails
- **Launch (cart open):** 7 days (T+0 to T+6), cart open, daily
  emails, social proof drops, masterclass replay if applicable
- **Sustain (mid-cart):** days 3-5 of the cart window, FAQ teardowns,
  objection-handling content, mid-cart proof posts
- **Close + optimise:** days 5-7 of the cart window, last-call
  emails, scarcity (REAL only), post-close offboarding sequence

Total: ~3 weeks ending at cart close, plus 1 week post-close for
post-mortem + waitlist-for-next-cohort capture.

## Override Notes

- If the coach has no email list yet, Stage 2 over-indexes on
  organic content + paid lead-magnet ads to build the list before
  the cart opens. Surface the gap at Stage 0.
- If the cohort cap is small (under 20 seats), Stage 4's kill
  criteria gets tighter: if <50% sold by mid-cart, audit the
  masterclass close.
- If the coach is in a regulated niche (financial advice, legal,
  health), surface compliance rules at Stage 0 and tighten the
  ban-list (no income claims, no medical claims, no legal-outcome
  claims).
