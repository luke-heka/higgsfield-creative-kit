## Brand

- **Name:** Selr AI
- **Category:** AI implementation + community for AU SMB operators and
  AI practitioners
- **Founder:** Selr AI founder + technical lead (co-deliver)
- **Voice:** Confident, direct, AU English, no hype. Operator-to-operator.
- **Language:** AU English (colour, optimise, specialise, organisation).

## Hero Offer (This Campaign Stub)

- **Offer:** In-person Workshop
- **Price:** $1,500 AUD
- **Format:** Full day, max 12 attendees, hands-on install
- **Cities:** Gold Coast (home), Melbourne, Sydney, one workshop per
  city per month
- **City for this demo:** Melbourne
- **Date for this demo:** Wednesday 2026-06-12

## Positioning One-Liner

"A hands-on workshop where 12 AU operators install one working AI
workflow in one room in one day. By 5pm you walk out with it running."

## What Selr AI Is NOT

- A course library or curriculum platform (we don't sell completion)
- A coaching program (we don't sell hours)
- An agency offering "done-for-you" (we don't sell deliverables)
- A retainer model (workshops are one-off; ASA is one-off; Skool is the
  ongoing layer)

## ICP Guesses (To Validate in Stage 1)

1. **AU SMB operators with a recurring ops bottleneck**, REPEAT
   (workshop → Skool conversion pipeline)
2. **Solo founders building AI agencies / consultancies**, AMPLIFIER
   (word-of-mouth in indie founder Slack / Discord / X)
3. **Mid-career operators repositioning into AI roles**, NEITHER
   (one-time workshop attendance, low Skool fit)
4. **Hospitality + trades operators (regional)**, REPEAT secondary
   (workshop → ASA pipeline if business size justifies)

## Campaign Goal

**Drive 12 paid workshop registrations for Melbourne 2026-06-12**,
followed by 40%+ Skool conversion from that cohort in the first 30 days
post-workshop.

## Visual Direction (Anchor)

- **Setting:** A real AU coworking workshop room. 12 chairs around 3
  trestle tables. Projector. Luke + Harvey at the front. Not a styled
  set.
- **Subjects:** AU operators mid-install on beat-up MacBooks. Real
  expressions, real focus. Not staged eye-contact with camera.
- **Lighting:** Mid-morning side window light, natural soft, overhead
  fluorescents switched off so the room feels human.
- **Camera energy:** Handheld with slight natural wobble. No tripod, no
  drone, no FPV.
- **Color palette anchors:**
  - `#1A1A1A` (dark wood / table / laptop bezel)
  - `#F5F1E8` (cream walls / paper / coffee cup ceramic)
  - `#7B61FF` (Selr purple, ACCENT ONLY, appears ONCE per frame)
- **Texture:** Fine film grain, natural skin texture, no AI shine.
- **Product visibility:** Selr purple as notebook spine OR lanyard OR
  coffee cup edge, appears once per frame, never centred, never
  background.

## Voice Direction (Anchor)

- **Tone words:** direct, dry, competent
- **Phrases to use:**
  - "in the room"
  - "by 5pm"
  - "you install it"
  - "we walk through it together"
  - "operator-to-operator"
  - "12 operators, one install each"
  - "you walk out with it running"
- **Phrases banned (Selr-specific):**
  - "transform your business"
  - "scale to 7 figures"
  - "AI-powered"
  - "next-level"
  - "game-changer"
  - "10x"
  - "unlock"
  - "elevate"
  - "supercharge"
  - "secret sauce"
  - "hidden growth lever"
- **Universal bans (apply across all Selr runs):**
  - Em dashes
  - Support promises ("we'll always be here", "weekly Q&A")
  - Outcome guarantees ("you'll grow X%", "guaranteed close rate")
  - Drop-in invites ("come say hi", "swing past")
  - Personal life ("my family", "my kids", "my morning routine")
  - Refund promises ("money-back", "30-day refund")
  - US English

## Hard Constraints

- No paid celebrity influencers
- No paid testimonials we can't disclose
- No US-centric examples in AU campaigns (no CrossFit Austin, no Silicon
  Valley founders) unless campaign explicitly targets US
- No "drop-in" workshop invites, every unpaid chat displaces a $1,500
  scoping call
- No refund promises in any sales material
- No personal life of Luke's in marketing
- Workshop is one-off, no "ongoing support" promised
- Skool is the ongoing layer, workshop close mentions it, doesn't bundle
  it into the workshop price

## Campaign Window Default

- Pre-launch: 7 days before workshop date (T-7 to T-1)
- Launch: workshop week (T+0 to T+6)
- Sustain: workshop + 7 to +14 days (Skool conversion sequence)
- Optimise: workshop + 14 to +21 days (re-cut for next city)

Total: 4 weeks ending 14 days after the workshop, before the next city
pre-launch.
