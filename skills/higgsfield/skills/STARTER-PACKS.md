# Starter Packs Index

Preconfigured business-owner-ready packages for the 6 Higgsfield Layer-2
sub-skills. Pick your industry, fill 3-5 fields, render. Real working ad
in under 10 minutes.

Every pack ships with:

- `brief.md`, preconfigured intake (variables in `{{double-braces}}`).
- `prompts.md`, paste-ready Higgsfield prompts in Skeleton 1/2 format.
- `sample/`, real Higgsfield render of the pack's lead prompt.
- `README.md`, 60-second how-to.

Six industries covered: **DTC ecommerce**, **personal trainer / fitness
coach**, **real estate agent**, **local service trade** (plumber,
electrician, café, hairdresser), **coach / course creator**, **SaaS
founder / indie hacker**.

## How packs work

Every pack is a self-contained folder under `<skill>/starter-packs/<industry>/`.
The pack does NOT replace the skill. It preconfigures the skill's inputs so
you don't have to write a brief from scratch.

To invoke any pack, open a fresh Claude Code session and paste the lead
prompt from that pack's `prompts.md`. The skill's normal workflow runs from
there, using the pack's `brief.md` as Stage 0 inputs.

Each pack hard-fails on its industry-specific compliance traps:

- DTC packs: no medical / cure claims for supps + skincare.
- PT packs: no specific weight-loss numbers.
- Real estate packs: AU CASA drone rules, no fabricated comps.
- Local-service packs: no specific response-time promises, no identifiable
  customer property in visuals, no phone-only CTAs.
- Coach packs: no income claims, no refund-language, no support promises.
- SaaS packs: no faked screen recordings (live screen recording is captured
  separately and composited in post).

---

## Voice rules across every pack

- No em dashes. Comma or full stop.
- No banned vocab in output copy (game-changer, 10x, crushing it, killing it,
  secret sauce, level up, transform, revolutionary).
- AU English for AU-default packs (PT, real estate, local trade,
  local-service-opening cmo). US English for DTC + SaaS default.
- No outcome guarantees. No support promises. No drop-in invites. No
  personal-life-in-marketing.
- Every text output passes through `content-engine` + `humanizer` ship-gate
  before saving.
