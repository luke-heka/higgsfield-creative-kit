# Local Service Trade, 3 Paste-Ready Seedance 2.0 Chunk Prompts

Three Skeleton-2 (video) prompts, one per stage of the 5-chunk script. Each is paste-ready into Higgsfield Seedance 2.0 after you swap the `{{VARIABLES}}` for the business's values.

Render settings for all three: **9:16, 720p, handheld selfie OR phone-on-bonnet-of-ute, Higgsfield "Change Voice" set to the same voice across all chunks.**

This file ships the **HOOK** (chunk 1), the **SOLUTION** (chunk 3), and the **CTA** (chunk 5). The `@owner` Element tag stays loaded across all 5 chunks. The `{{ASSET_TAG}}` (`@truck`, `@kitchen`, `@chair`) is loaded only for chunks 3, 4, 5.

## Notes for the operator

- **Voice consistency:** lock the voice on chunk 1 (warm Australian, conversational, no scripted radio voice). Hit **Change Voice** on every subsequent chunk and pick the SAME voice. Daniel works well for blokey trades, Megan or a warm female voice works well for hospo and salon.
- **Two tags, not one.** `@owner` stays loaded all 5 chunks. `{{ASSET_TAG}}` (`@truck`, `@kitchen`, `@chair`) is REMOVED for chunks 1 and 2, LOADED for chunks 3, 4, 5. Get this wrong and chunk 1 has a random ute in the corner that doesn't match your real one.
- **Word counts:** hook ≤16 words, solution ≤25 words, CTA ≤18 words. Slightly higher than DTC because trade/hospo voices carry longer lines and the AU phrasing adds words.
- **No false response-time promises.** "Same-day callouts" is a USP only if you actually deliver it consistently. Default `{{USP}}` to phrasing you can back: "transparent pricing", "no booking needed before 10am", "family-owned since 1998".
- **AU spelling:** these prompts use "colours" (AU) deliberately. The voiceover uses Australian phrasing ("mob", "turn up", "sorted"). If the business is US-based, swap to US spelling and drop the AU slang.
- **Filter discipline:** chunk 2 ("problem") describes competitor failure ("most mobs don't show up"). This can filter-fail on Seedance because it reads as comparative advertising. If it instant-fails, rewrite to describe the SCENE (e.g. "empty driveway at 4pm where the tradie was meant to be") instead of the comparison.
- **Customer property:** never show identifiable customer property in any chunk. If you swap in real worksite B-roll for chunk 4, mask plate numbers (CapCut blur tool) and any street signs.
- **Drone shots:** the EXCLUDE list bans drone over private property because of CASA rules in Australia. If you're doing a drone-over-shopfront shot of your own business, that's fine, override the exclude.
