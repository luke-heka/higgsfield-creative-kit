## Notes for the operator

- **Voice consistency:** lock the voice on chunk 1 then hit **Change Voice** on every subsequent chunk and pick the SAME voice. Australian or warm-neutral works best, avoid an American gym-bro voice.
- **The trainer IS the product.** No `@product` tag in this pack. `@trainer` is a character tag, loaded for all 5 chunks. Do not toggle.
- **Word counts:** hook ≤14 words, solution ≤22 words, CTA ≤16 words. Slightly higher caps than DTC because the trainer's pacing carries longer lines naturally.
- **Banned vocab check:** "transform", "level up", "crush it", "killing it" are all banned in fitness copy because they trigger Meta's auto-rejection on outcome promises. The voiceovers above use grounded language ("results", "strength building", "feels easier").
- **No weight-loss numbers.** If you swap copy and introduce "lose 10kg" or similar, the ad gets auto-rejected on Meta. Use feeling-based outcomes ("walking up stairs", "carrying the kids", "still energy at 5pm").
- **Filter discipline:** chunk 4 ("proof") with a real client first name will pass filter cleanly. If you swap in a number-based claim, it may filter-fail. Stay in feeling-language.
- **Background:** real Australian gym beats a generic American gym backdrop. Add gym-specific cues like "Aussie commercial gym" or name a chain ("Snap Fitness", "Anytime Fitness") if rights allow.
